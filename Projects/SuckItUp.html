Suck it Up

Suck it Up is a large team project (25 people) exploring cooperative mechanics and emergent enemy behaviours. I implemented the crawler enemy, including procedural animation blending and a custom navigation system tuned for irregular geometry.

  • Procedural animation
  • Custom navigation
  • Enemy design
  • Team collaboration (25 people)

Project Details

Duration: Semester
Key responsibilities:

  • Integration with gameplay systems

Crawler Enemy

The crawler uses layered procedural animation to combine locomotion, climbing and attack states. I designed a state machine with blending driven by environment probes and root motion correction for reliable foot placement on uneven surfaces.

Navigation

Default navmesh solutions were insufficient for the segmented, deformable geometry in the level. I built a custom local navigation layer that performs clearance sampling and dynamic obstacle avoidance, improving traversal success on complex geometry.

Collaboration

I coordinated with animators and level designers to ensure the procedural behaviors matched intended pacing and challenge, and helped onboard multiple teammates to the navigation tooling I developed.
